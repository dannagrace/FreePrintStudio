FreePrint Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

FreePrint Studio helps you prepare selected images for exact-size PDF export and AirPrint. The app is designed to work locally on your device.

Data Collection

FreePrint Studio does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data. The app does not create accounts and does not ask you to sign in.

Images and Documents

Images you choose are processed on your device for preview, PDF export, and printing. Selected images are not uploaded to a server by FreePrint Studio.

Tracking, Analytics, and Advertising

FreePrint Studio does not use tracking, third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or behavioral profiling.

Support Contact

If you contact the developer outside the app for support, information you choose to provide is used only to respond to your request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
